SEO Toolkit for Artists

1. SEO Basics for Artists

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) helps your website and artwork appear in search results. It’s how curators, journalists, collectors, and new audiences find you. SEO works best when your content is specific, relevant, and uses the right language.

2. Keyword Research Tools

  • Google Trends: Find what’s trending and compare keyword popularity by region.
  • Ubersuggest: Discover keyword suggestions and search volume.
  • AnswerThePublic: Visualize the questions people are asking around your keywords.
  • Google Search Autocomplete: Type in your topic and see what Google suggests.
  • Ask your peers: What would you type into Google to find someone like me?

3. Writing with SEO in Mind

Use keywords in:

  • Page titles (H1)
  • Meta descriptions
  • Image alt text
  • Artwork descriptions
  • Blog posts

Use plain, descriptive language. Avoid poetic titles without context. Instead of “Contemporary Art,” try: “Contemporary Art Miami/Contemporary Artist Miami.”

4. Website Optimization Checklist

  • Update favicon: make your own or find one online
  • Unique title + description for each page
  • Clear headers (H1 for titles, H2 for subtitles)
  • Internal links between pages (like About → Portfolio)
  • Alt text on all images + renamed image files with keywords

5. Free SEO Tools You Can Use

  • Google Search Console: Monitor how Google sees your site.
  • SEO Site Checkup: Perform a quick audit of your SEO health.
  • Yoast SEO or Rank Math (for WordPress users): Easy ways to optimize posts.
  • Wix SEO Wizard: Helps you edit SEO info step-by-step.
  • Squarespace SEO Settings: Accessible through page settings and image blocks.
  • Moz: Industry-standard tools to track rankings, perform keyword research, and more.

6. Beyond Your Website

  • Backlinks: Get other websites to link to you (articles, interviews, exhibitions)
  • Social Media: Post links to your website in captions, bio, and stories
  • Online Directories: Submit to artist directories like Artsy, ArtRabbit, or regional sites
  • Newsletters: Encourage clicks back to your website

7. AI Prompts to Help with SEO

  • “Make an SEO checklist for updating my [Wix/Squarespace/WordPress] artist website.”
  • “What keywords would a curator use to describe a Latin American digital artist?”
  • “Rewrite this exhibition description to improve SEO.”
  • Create alt text for these images of my work: [paste file names or short image descriptions].

8. SEO Best Practices for Artists

  • Be specific, not vague. Use descriptors that match how others search.
  • Use curators words about your work to make it more googleable.
  • Update your website regularly (Google likes fresh content).
  • Think like a stranger, not like yourself. What would someone unfamiliar search for?
  • Balance creativity with clarity—Google can’t guess what your poetic title means.
