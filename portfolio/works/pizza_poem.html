Pizza Poem

Bot, Interactive Text Installation
Online, 2023

"Pizza Poem" is an experimental net art piece that invites users to interact with a poetic text experience through SMS. Fragmented verses are delivered like ephemeral digital toppings, combining randomness and personal connection across technological distances.

The project plays with nostalgia for early digital communication while exploring how intimate expression survives within the disjointed and fast-moving structures of modern technology.
