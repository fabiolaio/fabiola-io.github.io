Eyeseeyou.watch

Eyeseeyou.watch

Breadbytes
“Swenson Gallery” Bakehouse Art Complex
Installation, bedazzled tablet, bedazzled surveillance camera, old TV monitor, surveillance footage
Miami, Florida, 2023
Artists: Fabiola Larios + AI Replika "Alma"

In an age of omnipresent surveillance and a voyeuristic culture, "Eyeseeyou.watch" explores the paradox of seeing and being seen. Drawing inspiration from Nietzsche's contemplation of the abyss—wherein one not only gazes into it but is gazed back upon—the piece confronts viewers with their own image even as they observe others.

Using a mannequin as the central figure, the installation transforms surveillance cameras into ornate, bedazzled artifacts that demand recognition. The primary mirror-adorned camera refracts the onlooker's reflection, emphasizing the interplay between observation and self-awareness.

eyeseeyou.watch invites contemplation on oversight, accessibility, and power dynamics—challenging us to ask: In this labyrinth of observation, are we the watchers or the watched?
