Heartware

Heartware

No Vacancy, Miami Beach
Riviera Suites South Beach
Installation — pink piñatas, lace, mirrors, embedded tablets and dash cameras, live video feeds, LED lighting, and CRT television
Miami Beach, Florida, 2025

Heartware brings together Mexican piñata traditions, the pink glow of Miami Vice, and the intimate textures of quinceañera dresses and lingerie. Suspended heart, star, and balloon-shaped piñatas become ornamental bodies that hold cameras, screens, and reflective surfaces.

Lace, mirrors, and pink lighting invite visitors to approach, while live video feeds bring their presence into the installation. The piñata, usually filled with treats and opened through a communal celebration, becomes a vessel for technologies that watch. Its familiar, festive appearance creates an encounter between affection, curiosity, and surveillance.

As part of my ongoing Cuteveillance research, Heartware examines how decoration and intimacy shape our relationship with surveillance technologies. The installation asks how easily an object associated with pleasure and celebration can also become an interface for observation.
