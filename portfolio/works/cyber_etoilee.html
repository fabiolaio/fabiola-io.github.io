Cyber Étoilée [FLOCK.exe]

Cyber Étoilée [FLOCK.exe]

Video Game in Raspberry Pi on 3D printed Cyberdeck
Pantin Art Center
Pantin, France, 2026

Cyber Étoilée is a portable cyberdeck that places a surveillance game inside a custom-built sculptural interface. The device brings together physical computing, ornament, and the visual language of an everyday monitoring system.

In FLOCK.exe, players scan and classify people within a pixel-art Miami. The interaction turns looking into an administrative action: a person becomes an image, a category, and a possible target. Refusing to participate triggers the profiling of the player, reversing the relationship between observer and observed.

The work asks how participation and refusal operate inside systems that continually produce information about us. The cyberdeck makes those questions tangible through a device that invites handling and play.
