Wild Wired World

Wild Wired World

Sea Change
Perez Art Museum Miami
3 min
3 channel video AI generated video collage, AI generated music
Miami, Florida, 2024

"Wild Wired World" is a video art project that immerses viewers in a surreal landscape of internet history, digital surveillance, and machine-generated memories. Constructed through AI-based visual storytelling, the piece reveals the chaotic beauty and latent dangers of a hyperconnected reality.

The project explores how personal data, nostalgia, and algorithmic structures intersect to create new hybrid memories—both artificial and hauntingly familiar.
